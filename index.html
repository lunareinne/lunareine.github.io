📕 My Little Recipe Book ♡

Red Velvet

Ingredients:
2 cups all-purpose flour
1½ teaspoons baking soda
½ teaspoon salt
1½ cups sugar
½ cup cocoa powder
3 large eggs, beaten
1 cup + 2 tablespoons oil
1½ cups grated cooked beets
2 teaspoons vanilla extract
8 oz cream cheese, softened
¾ cup powdered sugar, or more to taste
1 stick butter, room temperature

Steps:
1. Prepare the oven
Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour a 9 × 13-inch baking pan.

2. Mix the dry ingredients
In a bowl, combine flour, baking soda, salt, sugar, and cocoa powder. Set aside.

3. Mix the wet ingredients
In another mixing bowl, combine the beaten eggs and oil. Beat in the vanilla until well blended.

4. Combine
Slowly add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture. Beat until well combined.

5. Add the beets
Stir in the grated cooked beets until evenly distributed.

6. Bake
Pour the batter into the prepared pan. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 25–35 minutes, or until the cake springs back when lightly touched.

7. Cool
Let the cake cool completely in the pan on a rack.

8. Make the frosting
Beat the softened cream cheese and room-temperature butter together. Add the powdered sugar. Beat until smooth and fluffy.

Spread over the completely cooled cake. Refrigerate until ready to eat.

Cinnamon Rolls

Ingredients:
⅔ cup (151 g) cold whole milk
2½ cups (300 g) bread flour
1 tsp (6 g) salt
2 tbsp (25 g) granulated sugar
2 tsp instant yeast
4 tbsp (57 g) unsalted butter, softened
1 tbsp (14 g) butter, melted
½ cup (107 g) packed light brown sugar
2 tbsp (14 g) bread flour
3–4 tsp (8–10 g) ground cinnamon
1/16 tsp salt
3 tbsp (42 g) butter, melted, divided
½ tsp vanilla extract
1/16 tsp salt
1½ cups (170 g) confectioners’ sugar, sifted
1–2 tbsp (14–28 g) milk, heavy cream, or buttermilk

Steps:
Make the Dough

Add the cold milk, flour, salt, sugar, yeast, and softened butter to the tangzhong.

Mix until the dough comes together.

Knead until the dough becomes smooth, elastic, and slightly tacky. This takes about 15 minutes by hand or 10–12 minutes with a mixer.

First Rise

Shape the dough into a ball and place it in a covered bowl. Let it rise for 60–90 minutes, until puffy.

Make the Filling

Mix together melted butter, brown sugar, bread flour, cinnamon, and salt until it resembles damp sand.

Place the dough on a lightly greased surface. Press or roll it into a 10 × 12-inch rectangle, about ½ inch thick.

Spread the cinnamon filling over the dough, leaving about ½ inch uncovered along one long edge.

Starting from the filled long side, roll the dough into a log. Divide it into 8 equal pieces.

Place the rolls on a lightly greased or parchment-lined baking sheet, leaving space between them.

Cover the rolls and let them rise for 30–60 minutes, until puffy.

Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).

Bake for 14–18 minutes, until lightly golden.

For extra-soft cinnamon rolls, bake closer to 14 minutes.

As soon as they come out of the oven, brush them with 1½ tbsp melted butter.

Let them cool for 10–15 minutes.

Mix together remaining melted butter, vanilla, salt, powdered sugar, and milk/cream/buttermilk.

Mix until smooth and adjust the liquid until you get your preferred consistency.

Spread the icing over the warm cinnamon rolls and enjoy! 🤎
